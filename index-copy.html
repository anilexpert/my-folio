You have different questions? Ask Away!

What’s your typical project workflow?

I take a user-centered approach, starting with discovery and research, followed by wireframing, high-fidelity design, prototyping, and finally, development. Each phase includes clear milestones and frequent feedback loops.

Do you provide post-launch support?

Yes, I offer flexible support and maintenance packages to ensure your product remains up-to-date, secure, and performs optimally after launch. We can tailor a plan that fits your needs.

How do you ensure accessibility?

I strictly follow Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) and conduct rigorous testing with screen readers and other assistive technologies throughout the design and development process to ensure an inclusive experience.

Can you work with my existing tech stack?

Absolutely. I am proficient with a wide range of technologies and can seamlessly adapt to your team's current tools and frameworks, ensuring a smooth and efficient integration.
