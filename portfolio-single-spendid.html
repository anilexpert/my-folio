Project overview

SPENDiD is a fintech platform that simplifies financial awareness by providing personalized budgeting and peer spending insights. Unlike traditional budgeting tools that rely solely on user inputs, SPENDiD integrates proprietary crowd-sourced spending data and predictive intelligence to help users benchmark their financial behaviors.

The challenge was to transform a complex financial intelligence engine into a user-friendly budgeting experience — enabling individuals and enterprises to make confident financial decisions based on relatable, real-world insights.

Problem Statement:

How might we make financial planning intuitive, empowering, and data-driven for users who struggle with traditional budgeting tools?

Methodologies

Our research uncovered a clear need for a smart, empathetic budgeting assistant — one that understands users’ real-world context and guides them gently toward financial improvement.

Competitive Analysis

Competitor Strengths Weaknesses Key Learnings
Mint Automated expense tracking, easy categorization Overwhelming UI, limited predictive insights Simplicity and automation are key
YNAB Strong budgeting discipline, educational Complex setup, not beginner-friendly Users need immediate, actionable insights
PocketGuard Easy-to-use interface, focus on disposable income Lack of deep analytics or peer benchmarking Users appreciate simplicity with guidance
Truebill Bill tracking and cancellation Focuses more on subscriptions than overall budgeting Balance automation with personal context

Key Takeaways

Challenge

Converting a data-heavy financial model into an intuitive, emotionally supportive experience that doesn't overwhelm users.

Lesson Learned

Simplicity builds trust in fintech. Users don't want more data — they want clear, relatable insights.

Final Outcome

SPENDiD successfully bridges financial intelligence with empathy, transforming budgeting from a chore into an empowering, data-driven experience.
